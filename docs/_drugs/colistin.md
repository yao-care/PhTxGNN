## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Colistin belongs to the polymyxin class, which disrupts the outer membrane of Gram-negative bacteria. This is a direct antibacterial action.

Postinfectious vasculitis is an immune-mediated inflammatory condition that follows an infection rather than an active bacterial infection itself. No established link connects colistin's antibacterial mechanism to this immune process. The model's high score therefore should not be read as mechanistic support. In addition, colistin's known kidney and nerve toxicity argues against its use in this setting.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the package insert for safety information.

Colistin is generally known to carry risks to the kidneys and nervous system. These risks are a particular concern in a condition that may already involve kidney injury, such as vasculitis. The data compiled for this report records no drug interaction entries.
