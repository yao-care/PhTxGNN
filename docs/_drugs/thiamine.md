## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Thiamine is generally known as a cofactor for carbohydrate-metabolism enzymes such as pyruvate dehydrogenase and transketolase.

For resistance to thyroid hormone, no mechanistic link is supported by the data. The score comes from the TxGNN model (deep learning) and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence. Resistance to thyroid hormone is a monogenic receptor defect, and no established pathway connects a vitamin cofactor to thyroid hormone receptor beta signalling. The prediction should be treated as a computational hypothesis, not a plausible therapeutic lead.

For the other predictions:
- **Primary hereditary glaucoma** (score 0.9940): no trials, no literature, and no demonstrated role for thiamine in the structural or genetic defects of aqueous outflow that drive this disease.
- **Open-angle glaucoma** (score 0.9936): the link is indirect and associative. Thiamine supports mitochondrial energy metabolism, which is plausibly relevant to retinal ganglion cell vulnerability, but a therapeutic role has not been shown.
