## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known class pharmacology, clonazepam is a GABA-A positive allosteric modulator. It may reduce sleep fragmentation and periodic limb movements during sleep, which are common features of restless legs syndrome.

Benzodiazepines, and clonazepam in particular, have long been used off-label for restless legs syndrome, mainly for sleep complaints. A historical overview (2024) notes that roughly one quarter of treated patients in a large survey received benzodiazepines, alone or combined with other therapies. In some Asian countries clonazepam is a commonly used option.

The evidence is of low certainty. The Cochrane-type review and the 2025 AASM guideline describe only limited benefit, and current guidance favors other drug classes (alpha-2-delta ligands) and iron correction over benzodiazepines. No registered trials were supplied, so there is no Phase 3 RCT support.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions are recorded. Major-level interactions include morphine and liposomal morphine (additive central nervous system and respiratory depression). Moderate-level interactions include bupropion, aprepitant, omeprazole, cimetidine, clarithromycin, dronabinol, metoclopramide, nabilone, opium and sibutramine.
- **Cautions for this use**: The long half-life of clonazepam raises concerns about next-day sedation, falls, cognitive effects, dependence and respiratory depression, particularly in older patients.
