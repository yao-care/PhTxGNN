## Everolimus: From Renal Cell Carcinoma to Liposarcoma
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Everolimus is an mTOR inhibitor, originally used for renal cell carcinoma, breast cancer and tuberous sclerosis-related conditions.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **liposarcoma**, with **1 clinical trial** (a Phase 2 combination study) and **4 publications** currently supporting this direction. The evidence is indirect, because the trial tests everolimus only in combination with ribociclib.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Everolimus inhibits mTORC1, a protein complex downstream of the PI3K/AKT pathway that controls cell growth and proliferation. Detailed mechanism-of-action data were not available in the data compiled for this report. The description here is based on its known drug class.

Dedifferentiated liposarcoma is characterized by CDK4/MDM2 amplification. Combining a CDK4/6 inhibitor (ribociclib) with an mTOR inhibitor (everolimus) is intended to limit compensatory pathway activation and cell-cycle progression. Both agents have shown synergistic growth inhibition in multiple tumour models. Preclinical sarcoma models also point to mTOR and cell-cycle pathway vulnerability.

Everolimus is already established for renal cancer and other tumours with mTOR pathway activity, so extension to another solid tumour is plausible. However, the clinical data so far come from a combination regimen, so the contribution of everolimus alone cannot be separated out.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold** (for liposarcoma)

**Rationale:**
- Liposarcoma evidence rests on one single-arm Phase 2 combination trial, so the effect of everolimus alone is unproven. The only local registration is cancelled.
- The other predicted indications do not change this picture:
  - Rhabdomyosarcoma has only early-phase, mixed-tumour data (L3).
  - The other rhabdomyosarcoma subtypes, ovarian myxoid liposarcoma and TFE3-fusion renal cell carcinoma have no direct evidence (L4–L5).

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from DOH-FDA Philippines (a blocking gap for safety screening).
- Mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank.
- Clarification of the cancelled registration status and whether a valid product is available locally.
- For liposarcoma: efficacy data for everolimus alone, or randomized evidence.
- A monitoring plan for myelosuppression, metabolic effects, lung toxicity and drug interactions.
