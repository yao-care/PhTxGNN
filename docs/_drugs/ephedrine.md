# Ephedrine: From Allergic Rhinitis and Related Upper Airway Conditions to Nasal Cavity Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Ephedrine is a sympathomimetic drug originally used for upper airway conditions such as allergic rhinitis, vasomotor rhinitis, the common cold and pharyngitis.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **nasal cavity disease**,
but there are **0 registered clinical trials** and only **2 publications**, both on combination products, so the evidence rests largely on the model prediction.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Ephedrine is a sympathomimetic with alpha-adrenergic agonist activity. It constricts blood vessels in the nasal mucosa and relieves congestion. Detailed mechanism-of-action data were not available in the data compiled for this report, so this description reflects general pharmacological knowledge.

Nasal cavity disease is closely related to the rhinitis, vasomotor rhinitis and nasopharyngitis indications that ephedrine already carries. The prediction is therefore mostly an extension of existing use, not a true repurposing candidate. The very high score (99.90%) probably reflects this overlap.

Other predicted indications are weaker:
- **Acute laryngopharyngitis** (score 99.90%, evidence level L5): Decongestion is plausible, but there are no trials or literature. The condition is usually self-limiting and infectious, and the benefit would be small compared with the cardiovascular and stimulant risks of ephedrine.
- **Trigeminal autonomic cephalalgia** (score 99.34%, evidence level L5): The link is weak and indirect. Sympathomimetics may worsen headache or raise blood pressure, so this needs mechanistic and safety validation first.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- The nasal cavity disease prediction is plausible but mostly overlaps with existing rhinitis uses. The only literature concerns combination products, and no clinical trials exist. The sole Philippines registration is cancelled, and the safety profile includes many knowledge-graph contraindications.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The package insert for warnings and contraindications (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data
- Studies that isolate ephedrine's effect in nasal cavity disease
- Clarification of whether a current Philippines registration or product is available
- A benefit-risk assessment against alternative decongestants, given the cardiovascular and stimulant risks
