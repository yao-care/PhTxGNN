## Dolutegravir: From HIV Infection to Feline Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Dolutegravir is an integrase strand transfer inhibitor, originally used to treat HIV infection and AIDS.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **feline acquired immunodeficiency syndrome (FIV)**,
but the evidence is thin: **0 clinical trials** and only **1 publication** (a veterinary study of combination therapy) support this direction.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, dolutegravir is an integrase strand transfer inhibitor (INSTI), and its efficacy against HIV has been established. Mechanistically, it may be applicable to feline immunodeficiency.

Feline immunodeficiency virus (FIV) is a lentivirus, like HIV, and encodes an integrase related to HIV-1 integrase. An INSTI could therefore plausibly block FIV integration into the host genome.

Caution is needed. The single available publication studied a combination regimen (dolutegravir, tenofovir and emtricitabine) in FIV-infected cats, so it does not establish the efficacy of dolutegravir on its own. The very high TxGNN score most likely reflects how closely this disease resembles HIV/AIDS in the knowledge graph, not independent clinical validation.

For context, the second-ranked prediction, simian immunodeficiency virus infection, has 14 publications. They are animal studies only, for example dolutegravir monotherapy in SIV-infected macaques (PMID 30381490), which showed in vivo antiviral pressure and selection of resistance mutations. No human trials exist for that indication either.

## Philippines Market Information

The only registration on record (PNF-0154, dolutegravir, single-ingredient) has been cancelled. No dosage form or approved indication text is available, and there are currently no valid registrations in the Philippines.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 62 interactions were identified. Those listed include several rated **Major**: polyvalent-cation products such as aluminum hydroxide, calcium salts (carbonate, acetate, citrate, gluconate, lactate, phosphate), magnesium salts (carbonate, chloride, citrate), magaldrate, kaolin, attapulgite and iron. These may reduce dolutegravir absorption, so dosing needs to be separated from such products. **Minor** interactions include aprepitant, cimetidine, clarithromycin, clotrimazole and dexamethasone.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction is supported only by a model score and one veterinary study of a combination regimen. There are no clinical trials, the drug has no valid Philippines registration, and the target is a veterinary condition rather than a human disease.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Confirmation that dolutegravir itself, not only the combination regimen, was tested against FIV, and dedicated efficacy and dosing studies in cats
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the DOH-FDA Philippines
- A clear decision on whether a veterinary indication is within the scope of this repurposing programme, and the regulatory and market status for it
