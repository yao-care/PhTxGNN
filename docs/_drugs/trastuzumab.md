# Trastuzumab: From HER2-Positive Breast Carcinoma to Progesterone-Receptor Positive Breast Cancer

## One-Sentence Summary

Trastuzumab is a HER2-targeted antibody originally used to treat HER2-positive breast carcinoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **progesterone-receptor positive breast cancer**, with **14 clinical trials** and **20 publications** currently supporting this direction.
This is best understood as a subpopulation of the original indication (HR+/HER2+ disease) rather than a true new use.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, trastuzumab is a monoclonal antibody that targets HER2 and is already approved for HER2-positive breast cancer.

Progesterone-receptor (PR) positivity is a hormone-receptor status that frequently co-occurs with HER2 positivity (HR+/HER2+ disease). The prediction therefore describes a subpopulation of the approved indication rather than a distinct disease. Combining HER2 blockade with endocrine therapy is a recognized strategy for these patients.

The benefit is driven by HER2 expression, not by PR status. The evidence does not extend to HR+/HER2-negative disease, so any use should be restricted to confirmed HER2-positive tumours.

## Safety Considerations

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Large Phase 3 data, including a completed adjuvant trial of 3,270 patients, support trastuzumab in HER2-positive breast cancer, which includes HR+/HER2+ disease. This is a subpopulation of the existing indication, not a new disease, so the benefit depends on HER2 status and not PR status.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Restrict use to confirmed HER2-positive disease; no benefit is expected in HR+/HER2-negative or triple-negative disease
- A currently valid Philippines registration (the only registration on record is cancelled)
- Package insert warnings and contraindications, which are not yet available
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- A cardiac monitoring plan and a review of the Major drug interactions

**Other predicted indications (for reference):**
- The "progesterone-receptor negative breast cancer" prediction (99.90%) and the "breast tumor luminal A or B" prediction (99.90%) also support Proceed with Guardrails, but only in HER2-positive disease. Luminal A is generally HER2-negative, so no HER2-directed benefit is expected.
- The "normal breast-like subtype" prediction is Hold (L4). The only related trial involves a different molecule (trastuzumab rezetecan) and has not started recruiting.
- Predictions ranked 5 to 10 (including malignant cutaneous granular cell tumour, ectomesenchymoma, HHV-8-related tumour, middle ear neuroendocrine tumour, and two urothelial carcinomas) have no clinical or literature evidence (L5) and are Hold.
