## Mercaptopurine: Myeloid leukemia

## One-Sentence Summary

Mercaptopurine is an oral purine antimetabolite chemotherapy drug that is marketed in the Philippines. The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **myeloid leukemia**, with a score of 0.9994. Support is limited: two small non-randomized AML studies use mercaptopurine directly, and the registered trials in this area do not show mercaptopurine as the variable being tested. The evidence level is **L3**.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Mercaptopurine is known as a purine antimetabolite. Its thioguanine nucleotide metabolites are incorporated into DNA and RNA and inhibit de novo purine synthesis. Rapidly dividing myeloid blasts depend heavily on nucleotide synthesis, so this mechanism is plausible for myeloid leukemia.

Published clinical use supports this link:

- A cohort study gave daily oral 6-mercaptopurine plus weekly methotrexate as maintenance to transplant-ineligible AML patients (PMID 26425037).
- A prospective pilot study tested metronomic oral 6-mercaptopurine in elderly AML (PMID 27275453).
- A multicentre report in childhood AML used ALL-like consolidation (PMID 38828002).

However, several Phase 3 trials appear under this disease term without showing that mercaptopurine is the tested variable. The direct evidence is therefore weaker than the trial phases suggest.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- Direct evidence for mercaptopurine in myeloid leukemia comes from small, non-randomized studies. No randomized trial tests mercaptopurine itself, and the listed Phase 3 trials do not show it as the tested drug.
- Mercaptopurine carries significant myelosuppression risk, so the benefit needs clearer confirmation before it moves forward.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Confirmation from full trial protocols of whether mercaptopurine is part of the regimens in the registered trials
- Prospective or randomized data on mercaptopurine maintenance in AML, including the venetoclax combination
- The package insert warnings and contraindications for the Philippine product
- A safety monitoring plan, including TPMT/NUDT15 testing and blood count follow-up
