## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, sumatriptan is a 5-HT1B/1D receptor agonist. It is thought to relieve migraine through cranial vasoconstriction and inhibition of trigeminal neuropeptide release (such as CGRP), which reduces trigeminovascular pain transmission.

Migraine with brainstem aura is a subtype of the same disorder that sumatriptan already treats. This close relationship is the main reason for the high prediction score. The score most likely reflects how near the two conditions sit in the knowledge graph, not independent proof of benefit.

There is also a caution. Triptans have traditionally been used carefully or avoided in aura subtypes with brainstem or motor features, such as hemiplegic and basilar-type migraine. The concern is theoretical ischaemia from vasoconstriction. The supplied data do not establish whether sumatriptan is safe and effective specifically for brainstem aura.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications flagged in the knowledge graph** (not package insert text): the knowledge graph links sumatriptan with cardiovascular and cerebrovascular conditions, including:
  - Coronary artery disease, myocardial infarction, heart disease and congestive heart failure
  - Hypertension
  - Stroke, cerebral infarction and cerebrovascular disorder
  - Peripheral vascular disease
  - Liver disease/failure and kidney disease
  - Epilepsy
  - Diabetes mellitus
  - Blood coagulation disease
  - Substance abuse/dependence

  These are consistent with the vasoconstriction concern in aura subtypes.
- **Drug Interactions** (100 interactions recorded; main ones shown):
  - Major: dexfenfluramine, fenfluramine, lorcaserin and sibutramine (serotonergic agents); dolasetron, granisetron, ondansetron and palonosetron (5-HT3 antagonists)
  - Moderate: morphine, including liposomal morphine

Package insert warnings and contraindications for the Philippines are not available, so please refer to the package insert for further safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- The prediction rests on model scoring and the drug's established role in migraine. There are no clinical trials and no literature specifically on brainstem aura. There are also theoretical ischaemic safety concerns for this subtype, and the Philippines registration is cancelled.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Subtype-specific clinical or observational data on sumatriptan in migraine with brainstem aura
- A cardiovascular and cerebrovascular safety assessment for aura subtypes with brainstem features
- Philippines package insert warnings and contraindications
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- A decision on re-registration or on an alternative product route, since no valid licence exists
