## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general azole pharmacology, miconazole inhibits fungal CYP51 (lanosterol 14-alpha-demethylase). It also has anti-inflammatory activity and activity against Gram-positive bacteria.

There are two plausible routes to benefit in acne:

- **Antibacterial:** A laboratory study (PMID 20045949) found that azole antifungals inhibit *Propionibacterium (Cutibacterium) acnes*, the bacterium involved in acne.
- **Antifungal and anti-inflammatory:** Miconazole acts against *Malassezia (Pityrosporum)*, which can cause folliculitis that mimics or aggravates acne. A review of miconazole's effects on skin disorders (PMID 18627330) describes anti-inflammatory effects that could help inflammatory lesions.

This link rests on general pharmacology rather than a curated target profile. The TxGNN score is a computational prediction only and is not clinical evidence.
