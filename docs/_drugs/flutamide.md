## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the compiled data. Flutamide is known to block the androgen receptor, so it prevents androgens from driving growth in androgen-responsive tissue.

The prostate depends on androgen signalling. Blocking the receptor could plausibly affect androgen-dependent stromal (connective) tissue in the gland, which is where a prostatic fibroma would arise. This is a theoretical link. No trial, case series or mechanistic study supports it, and the high score reflects similarity to other prostate conditions in the model, not demonstrated benefit.
