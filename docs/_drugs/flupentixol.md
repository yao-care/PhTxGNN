## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Flupentixol is known to be a thioxanthene antipsychotic that acts as an antagonist at dopamine receptors. Pharmacology records list activity at the D1, D2, D3 and D5 receptors.

No mechanistic link to the predicted disease can be established from the available data. Retinal dystrophies are genetically heterogeneous conditions in which photoreceptors or the retinal pigment epithelium degenerate. There is no plausible rationale for dopamine receptor blockade to change that process. The score of 0.9999 is a model-based computational output only and should not be read as evidence of benefit.
