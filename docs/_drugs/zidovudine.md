## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Zidovudine is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NRTI). Its efficacy in HIV infection and AIDS is established, and it is mechanistically plausible for other lentiviruses that depend on reverse transcriptase.

Feline immunodeficiency virus (FIV) is a lentivirus that causes an AIDS-like disease in cats. It is widely used as an animal model of HIV. Zidovudine has been tested in infected cats for many years, so the model's prediction fits the drug's known antiviral mechanism.

The prediction is not relevant to human drug repurposing, because it concerns a veterinary disease. Some reports also describe zidovudine toxicity in cats.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease associations from the knowledge graph (not label text):** The TxGNN knowledge graph links zidovudine to contraindication-type associations with the following conditions.
  - Blood: neutropenia, agranulocytosis, anemia (including macrocytic anemia).
  - Liver and metabolic: liver failure, liver disease, fatty liver disease, non-alcoholic steatohepatitis, lactic acidosis, hypertriglyceridemia, obesity disorders.
  - Muscle: myopathy, muscular disease, skeletal muscle disease, myositis, dermatomyositis, familial periodic paralysis.
  - Other: kidney disease, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, pyoureter.
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were found. The main ones among those reviewed are:
  - Moderate: amphotericin B (including the cholesteryl sulfate and lipid complex forms), corticosteroids (betamethasone, budesonide, dexamethasone, hydrocortisone, prednisolone, prednisone, triamcinolone), statins (rosuvastatin, simvastatin), naltrexone and orlistat.
  - Minor: cimetidine and clarithromycin.
  - Unknown level: pantoprazole, morphine, metformin and omeprazole.
- **Animal toxicity:** Zidovudine toxicity has been reported in FeLV-infected cats.

Please also refer to the package insert for safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- The top prediction is a feline disease supported only by veterinary and animal-model studies, with no registered human trials, so it is not a viable human repurposing candidate.
- The Philippine registration is cancelled, and the package insert warnings are not available.
- Two lower-ranked predictions are within zidovudine's established HIV use rather than new repurposing:
  - AIDS-related complex has Phase 3 support (NCT00001011, n=538).
  - Congenital HIV has Phase 3 support (NCT00102960, n=377), plus U.S. Public Health Service recommendations on perinatal use.
  - Both carry Proceed with Guardrails ratings. Hematologic toxicity, myopathy, pregnancy safety, and use inside combination therapy rather than monotherapy would need attention.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Philippine FDA.
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank.
- Confirmation of whether any human indication is worth pursuing, given that the human predictions are on-label HIV uses.
- Clarification of the current regulatory status, since the only registration is cancelled.
