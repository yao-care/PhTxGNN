## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Rilpivirine is known as a non-nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NNRTI) used against HIV-1. Its relation to HIV infection and AIDS is recorded in the knowledge graph.

SIV and SHIV (a chimeric virus carrying HIV-1 reverse transcriptase) are the standard macaque models of HIV. Activity in these models is mostly a translational proxy for the HIV use, not a truly new human indication. SIV is naturally resistant to many NNRTIs, so the studies mostly use SHIV chimeras.

Under the strict evidence rules, the cited studies are macaque studies of SHIV/RT-SHIV or SIV models. Because they are not direct studies of SIV infection with rilpivirine as a distinct disease target, and no clinical trials exist, the evidence level is capped at L5.

## Safety Considerations

- **Relations recorded in the knowledge graph (not label text)**: The knowledge graph links rilpivirine with contraindication relations to long QT syndrome (including familial), torsades de pointes, liver failure, liver disease, fatty liver disease, non-alcoholic steatohepatitis, kidney disease, gallbladder disease, chronic hepatitis B virus infection, and several others. These must be checked against the official label.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found. Major ones listed include famotidine, ranitidine, cimetidine, omeprazole, rabeprazole, esomeprazole, dexlansoprazole (acid-reducing agents), dexamethasone, dolasetron, and cisapride. Moderate ones include loperamide, aprepitant, clarithromycin, and antacids/laxatives such as aluminum hydroxide, calcium carbonate and magnesium oxide.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests on animal-model studies that mainly reflect the existing HIV use. There are no clinical trials, and SIV is not a distinct human disease target. The evidence level is L5.

- A clear case for what unmet need SIV infection (a non-human disease) would address
