## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Ethinylestradiol is a synthetic estrogen, and estrogen status is known to influence circulating trace-element levels. For example, oral contraceptives have been reported to change serum zinc and copper.

This is a plausible indirect physiological association, not a validated therapeutic mechanism. Elevated plasma zinc is a laboratory finding, often related to excess zinc intake, rather than a disease usually treated with a drug. The direction of any effect (lowering or raising zinc) and its clinical relevance are therefore unclear. The missing mechanism data further limits assessment.
