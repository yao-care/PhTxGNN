## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications suggested by the knowledge graph** (these are not label text and need verification against the package insert): hypertension, epilepsy, anxiety and neurotic disorders, porphyria, kidney disease, liver disease, gallbladder disease, hyperglycaemia and diabetes mellitus (including diabetic ketoacidosis), hypothyroidism, systemic lupus erythematosus, asthma, hypertriglyceridaemia, hypercalcaemia, and female breast carcinoma.
- **Drug Interactions**: The database lists 100 interactions; the first 20 reviewed are all of moderate severity. They are mainly with glucose-lowering agents (acarbose, metformin, dapagliflozin, linagliptin, nateglinide, GLP-1 agonists and several insulins), plus aprepitant, clotrimazole, metreleptin and prucalopride. Blood glucose monitoring is advisable when co-administered with antidiabetic drugs.
