# Captopril: From Hypertension to Malignant Renovascular Hypertension

## One-Sentence Summary

Captopril is an ACE inhibitor originally used to treat hypertension and diabetic nephropathy.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **malignant renovascular hypertension**,
but there are currently **no registered clinical trials** and the **20 publications** retrieved are mostly diagnostic reports, reviews, or case reports rather than treatment studies.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Captopril is known as an ACE inhibitor, which blocks the renin-angiotensin system (RAS). Its efficacy in hypertension and diabetic nephropathy is established.

Renovascular hypertension, particularly the malignant form, is driven by RAS activation after reduced blood flow to the kidney. Blocking this system is therefore mechanistically plausible. Captopril is already used diagnostically (captopril renography) and has historical use in severe or malignant hypertension.

There is an important caution. ACE inhibitors can cause acute kidney function decline in patients with bilateral renal artery stenosis or stenosis in a solitary kidney. The high TxGNN score largely reflects overlap with captopril's existing hypertension indications and is not new evidence by itself.

Other predictions in the data are weaker:
- **Malignant hypertensive renal disease** (score 99.28%, L5): the only publication is a diagnostic case report, with no treatment data.
- **Pulmonary hypertension owing to lung disease and/or hypoxia** (99.15%, L5): the retrieved literature is largely off-target, and ACE inhibitors have not shown reliable benefit here.
- **Pulmonary hypertension with unclear multifactorial mechanism** (99.15%, L5): no trials and no literature.

## Philippines Market Information

No currently valid registration exists. The only record is a cancelled single-ingredient registration (PNF-0073, product name Captopril), with no dosage form or approved indication text on file.

## Safety Considerations

Captopril package insert warnings and contraindications were not available in the data compiled for this report, so please refer to the package insert.

- **Contraindications flagged in the knowledge graph** (not label text): kidney disease, liver disease, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, angioedema (including hereditary, acquired, and RAAS-blocker-induced forms), systemic lupus erythematosus, diabetes mellitus and diabetic ketoacidosis, neutropenia and agranulocytosis, aortic stenosis, hypotensive disorders, hyperparathyroidism, hepatic coma/encephalopathy, connective tissue disease, potassium deficiency, and bone disease.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were recorded. Most listed ones are Moderate, including:
  - corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, triamcinolone, budesonide)
  - antidiabetic agents (metformin, alogliptin, canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, ertugliflozin, exenatide, chlorpropamide, acetohexamide)
  - acetylsalicylic acid, morphine, bupropion, dronabinol

  Antacids (aluminum hydroxide, calcium carbonate) are Minor.
- **Clinical consideration**: in this setting, the risk of acute renal function decline with bilateral renal artery stenosis or stenosis in a solitary kidney is the main concern.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction is mechanistically plausible for malignant renovascular hypertension, but no clinical trials exist and the literature is diagnostic or descriptive rather than therapeutic (L4). The product also has no currently valid registration in the Philippines.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Treatment-focused clinical evidence (trials or well-designed cohort studies) in malignant renovascular hypertension
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- The Philippines package insert warnings and contraindications
- A renal safety plan, including screening for bilateral renal artery stenosis and monitoring of renal function and potassium
- Clarification of the cancelled registration status and any need for re-registration
