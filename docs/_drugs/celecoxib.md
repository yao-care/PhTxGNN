# Celecoxib: From Arthritis and Spondyloarthropathy to Inflammatory Spondylopathy

## One-Sentence Summary

Celecoxib is a COX-2 inhibitor originally used for rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, ankylosing spondylitis and juvenile idiopathic arthritis.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **inflammatory spondylopathy**, the highest-evidence prediction among the top 10 candidates, with **11 clinical trials** and **20 publications** currently supporting this direction.
Because ankylosing spondylitis and spondyloarthropathy are already among its original indications, this is largely an extension of established use rather than a new repurposing.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, celecoxib is a selective COX-2 inhibitor. It reduces prostaglandin-mediated inflammation and pain, and its efficacy in arthritis and ankylosing spondylitis is established.

Inflammatory spondylopathy covers spondyloarthritis conditions, including ankylosing spondylitis and axial spondyloarthritis. These overlap with the spondyloarthropathy and ankylosing spondylitis indications celecoxib already carries. The predicted use is therefore mechanistically consistent with established use.

Recent literature also suggests celecoxib may inhibit radiographic bone progression in spondyloarthritis, possibly by modulating prostaglandin-driven new bone formation. This requires confirmation. Any use should be symptomatic and should be guarded against cardiovascular, gastrointestinal and renal risk.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease-related cautions (from the knowledge graph, not label text):** Celecoxib is linked to contraindications or cautions in cardiovascular disease (including hypertension, congestive heart failure, myocardial infarction and stroke), kidney disease, liver disease, gallbladder disease, active peptic ulcer disease, asthma, anaemia and hemoglobinopathy, and substance abuse/dependence.
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were found in total. One is rated Major: **eliglustat**. Many are rated Moderate, including hydrocortisone, metformin, sulfonylureas (glimepiride, glipizide, glyburide, chlorpropamide, acetohexamide), nateglinide, exenatide, mesalazine, balsalazide, olsalazine, cimetidine, levofloxacin, metronidazole, kanamycin and naltrexone.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
- Two completed Phase 3 randomized trials (NCT00648141, NCT00762463), several Phase 4 trials and a 2024 RCT support celecoxib in ankylosing spondylitis and axial spondyloarthritis, which meets the L1 criteria. This use largely overlaps with its existing indications. Cardiovascular, gastrointestinal and renal risks require guardrails.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Philippines package insert warnings and contraindications (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Confirmation of the regulatory pathway: the only Philippines registration (PNF-0091) is cancelled and has no indication text, so a new or renewed registration would be needed
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Confirmation that "inflammatory spondylopathy" maps to the ankylosing spondylitis and axial spondyloarthritis populations studied in the trials
- A cardiovascular, GI and renal risk screening and monitoring plan, with attention to the major eliglustat interaction
- Confirmation of the radiographic progression benefit
