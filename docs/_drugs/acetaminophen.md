## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications (TxGNN model associations, not label text)**: The TxGNN model (deep learning) associates acetaminophen with contraindications in liver failure, liver disease, cirrhosis of the liver, hepatic encephalopathy, hepatic coma, and gallbladder disease. These associations may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found in total. The 20 shown in the data are mostly Minor. Moderate-level interactions are with:
  - Activated charcoal
  - Glycerol phenylbutyrate
  - Naltrexone

  Minor-level interactions include anticholinergic agents (atropine, scopolamine, hyoscyamine, dicyclomine, glycopyrronium), H2 blockers (cimetidine, ranitidine), metoclopramide, and liraglutide. Vitamin A and calcitriol are listed with an unknown level.
