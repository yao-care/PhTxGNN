## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacology, pilocarpine is a muscarinic (M3) receptor agonist. It contracts the ciliary muscle and increases trabecular outflow, which lowers intraocular pressure (IOP). Because elevated IOP is the main treatable feature of hereditary glaucoma, there is a plausible link. This mechanism is inferred from general knowledge and not from the compiled record.

The original indication (open-angle glaucoma) and the predicted indication (primary hereditary glaucoma) are closely related, so the prediction is largely a re-expression of an existing use and adds little new repurposing information.

There are also important caveats. Primary hereditary glaucoma (for example, primary congenital glaucoma, often linked to CYP1B1 or MYOC variants) typically arises from developmental abnormalities of the trabecular meshwork or drainage angle. Miotics such as pilocarpine may be less effective there than in adult open-angle disease, and may even act paradoxically in some paediatric or angle-dysgenesis cases. This is a general pharmacological caution and is not supported by data in the compiled record.

  - Kidney and urinary: nephrolithiasis, SLC26A1-related nephrolithiasis susceptibility, acute urate nephropathy
  - Liver and biliary: liver failure, cholelithiasis, cholecystolithiasis, choledocholithiasis, cholecystitis, low phospholipid-associated cholelithiasis, biliary tract disease, obstructive jaundice

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The TxGNN score is very high, but there are no clinical trials or publications behind it (evidence level L5). The prediction mostly restates the existing glaucoma indication, and pilocarpine may be less effective, or even counterproductive, in developmental forms of hereditary glaucoma. In the Philippines, the only registration is cancelled, so the drug has no currently valid marketing authorization.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the DOH-FDA Philippines (currently a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Confirmed mechanism of action data from DrugBank

- Clarification of the cancelled registration status and dosage form (ophthalmic route compatibility has not been assessed)
- Clinical assessment of whether the prediction adds value beyond the existing open-angle glaucoma indication
