## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications flagged in the knowledge graph** (drug–disease contraindication links, not package-insert text):
  - Eye: angle-closure glaucoma, closed-angle glaucoma, keratitis, keratoconjunctivitis, iritis, uveitis, iridocyclitis, retinal detachment
  - Respiratory: asthma, bronchial disease, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (including severe early-onset COPD)
  - Kidney and urinary: nephrolithiasis, SLC26A1-related nephrolithiasis susceptibility, acute urate nephropathy
  - Liver and biliary: liver failure, cholelithiasis, cholecystolithiasis, choledocholithiasis, cholecystitis, low phospholipid-associated cholelithiasis, biliary tract disease, obstructive jaundice
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found in total. The main ones listed are:
  - Moderate level: anticholinergic or antimuscarinic agents, namely hyoscyamine, atropine (including ophthalmic), glycopyrronium (including topical), clidinium, dicyclomine, mepenzolate, methscopolamine, propantheline, scopolamine (including ophthalmic) and trospium. These are likely to oppose pilocarpine's cholinergic effect.
  - Level unknown: pantoprazole, clotrimazole, morphine, metformin, omeprazole, lansoprazole and epinephrine.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.
