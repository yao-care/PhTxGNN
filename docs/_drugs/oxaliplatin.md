## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Oxaliplatin is a third-generation platinum compound that forms DNA crosslinks, which blocks DNA replication and transcription and leads to cancer cell death. Its efficacy in colorectal cancer is well established.

Platinum drugs such as cisplatin are the backbone of standard chemotherapy for mesothelioma, so a shared mechanism is biologically plausible. Oxaliplatin has been tested in mesothelioma in combination with gemcitabine, raltitrexed, vinorelbine and bortezomib. Phase 2 studies report modest activity. This is consistent with the very high TxGNN score.

Evidence is not uniformly positive. One small second-line study of raltitrexed plus oxaliplatin (14 patients) closed early because it produced no objective responses. The other mesothelioma subtypes predicted by the model (epithelioid, sarcomatoid, visceral pleura tumour) rest mainly on indirect evidence such as peritoneal HIPEC/PIPAC studies. Their scores likely reflect disease-ontology proximity rather than independent evidence.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications (from the knowledge graph, not label text):** The knowledge graph links oxaliplatin to caution in kidney disease, liver disease, gallbladder disease, peripheral nervous system disease and peripheral neuropathy, interstitial lung disease and pulmonary fibrosis, and pyoureter.
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were identified. Among those listed, one is **Major** and the rest are Moderate.
  - **Major:** dolasetron.
  - **Moderate (examples):** famotidine, amphotericin B, loperamide, mesalazine, balsalazide, bisacodyl, metronidazole, clarithromycin, levofloxacin, vancomycin, kanamycin, palonosetron, naltrexone, lactulose.

For other warnings, please refer to the package insert.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Two Phase 2 trials (one completed, one of unknown status) and several Phase 2 publications support oxaliplatin combinations in pleural mesothelioma. However, all are small, single-arm studies with modest or mixed results, and no Phase 3 data exist. The only Philippines registration is cancelled, and no package insert safety data have been obtained. The evidence supports further research, not immediate use.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Philippines FDA, which is a blocking gap for safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Confirmation of whether an active oxaliplatin product can be registered or supplied in the Philippines
- Results of the completed Phase 2 trial (NCT00859469) and comparison against standard platinum-pemetrexed therapy
- Subtype-specific evidence for epithelioid, sarcomatoid and visceral pleura tumours before considering those indications
