## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for folic acid are not available in the data compiled for this report. In general, folate acts as a coenzyme in one-carbon metabolism (methyl-group transfer and amino acid and nucleotide metabolism). Biotin acts as a coenzyme for carboxylase enzymes. Both are B vitamins, and their metabolic pathways can intersect, which is a plausible reason for the model to link them.

This link is weak. The retrieved publications cover B vitamins in general (vitamin-responsive disorders, diabetes, dermatology, nutrition). None shows that folic acid treats biotin metabolic diseases such as biotinidase deficiency or holocarboxylase synthetase deficiency. These disorders are treated with biotin itself. A direct therapeutic role for folic acid is therefore not established, and the high score should be read as a network-based signal only.
