## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Based on known information, lynestrenol is a progestin prodrug that is converted in the body to norethisterone. It suppresses ovulation and can steady hormonal fluctuations.

Menstrual-related migraine is thought to be triggered by the fall in estrogen in the late luteal phase. A hormone-stabilizing drug could therefore plausibly reduce hormone-sensitive migraine, which is consistent with the high TxGNN score. This link is inferred. It has not been demonstrated for lynestrenol in modern studies.

A caution applies. Combined estrogen-containing contraceptives are contraindicated in migraine with aura because of stroke risk. A progestin-only agent may differ, but no data in this report address that question.
